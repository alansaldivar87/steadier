# We can start small

You do not need to organize every debt before beginning. Work through one batch at a time, for both partners. Upload through the app; you can also keep source documents in the ignored `intake/private/` folder locally.

## Batch 1: protect the home and the next payday

- Latest mortgage statement, missed-payment notice, forbearance agreement, and any repayment proposal.
- Any explicit application deadline, legal notice or sale date, copied exactly from the source.
- Current available cash account balances and pending automatic withdrawals.
- Each person's next take-home paycheck: amount and date.
- Essential bills due before those paydays.

Use `HOUSING-FIRST.md` to prepare for a servicer call. Contact the servicer now if housing is overdue; do not wait for the rest of this intake.

## Batch 2: list everything owed

For each mortgage, card, loan, consolidation account, medical balance, buy-now-pay-later plan, personal obligation and cash advance app, collect:

- Provider and account nickname (last four digits are enough).
- Latest balance and its date.
- APR if known; promotional expiration, fees, and deferred interest terms if applicable.
- Minimum payment, next due date and past-due amount.
- Automatic debit dates and paying account.
- Forbearance, collections or agreement status and written terms.

Start a debt record even when APR is unknown. Cash advance fees and tips should be recorded as actual fees, not guessed APR. Add all expected repayments to the calendar.

## Batch 3: discover spending

- Three months of checking, savings and credit card statements, preferably CSV plus statement PDFs.
- Cash receipts and payments that never appeared in a bank account.
- Subscriptions, insurance, utilities, rent/mortgage, groceries, transportation, childcare and irregular annual bills.
- Transfers between your accounts, which should not be counted as spending twice.

Start accounts using each first statement's opening balance/date, then import transactions on or after that date. For CSV, use `Date,Description,Amount` with negative money out and positive money in; `Debit,Credit` columns are also supported. Review transfer/refund/borrowing classifications. Compare closing balances to the ledger.

## Every day (about a minute)

Capture a receipt, type a purchase, or tell the coach what happened. Confirm its amount, date, account, and category. If the bank entry arrives later, match/dismiss the duplicate.

## Together once a week

Review outstanding drafts and statement differences. Check what is due before the next payday. Update loan balances when statements arrive. Agree on one small spending change and one next action. Use plain category names you both recognize.

Do not mark setup complete until all known paydays, essential expenses and debt withdrawals are represented. Missing information is expected; it should stay visible.
