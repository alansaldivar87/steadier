# Prepare the mortgage servicer call

For U.S. mortgages, the CFPB advises contacting the servicer right away when a payment is missed or at risk. A HUD-approved housing counselor can help with options and documentation. If foreclosure is imminent or legal papers were served, local legal advice may also be needed. [CFPB guidance, checked September 10, 2026](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/if-i-cant-pay-my-mortgage-loan-what-are-my-options-en-268/).

## Opening script

“I missed a payment after forbearance and want to resolve this. Please connect me with the team that handles mortgage assistance or loss mitigation. I need to understand my current status, the exact amount due, any deadlines, and the assistance options available for my loan. I can provide income and expense information.”

## Ask and record

- What is the account's current status and the exact past-due breakdown?
- Has foreclosure action begun, and is there a date or deadline I need to address immediately?
- Which repayment or hardship options can you review for this loan?
- What documents and application are required, and where should I submit them?
- What would each proposed payment be, on which dates, for how long?
- How would fees, interest, escrow and credit reporting be handled?
- What happens if a proposed payment is missed?
- Please send the proposed agreement and requirements in writing.

Do not promise a payment based on an incomplete forecast. Compare the actual written proposal against household income and essential expenses before accepting.
